*Let's Go Somewhere Else* is an interactive piece that explores the creation of the self in the digital world.

The experience is built on the architecture of the Internet itself, turning pop-up windows into horror stories about dysmorphia, paranoia and desire in the corporate technological complex. Like the Internet, what at first appears fragmented and tethered to the ephemeral builds into a narrative of longing, connection and the hope of possible tomorrows.
