*MÀQUINX DE HACER MÀQUINXS* (Machine for Making Machines) is an experimental piece exploring the recursive nature of creation and the relationship between human and machine intelligence. This work investigates how tools create tools, how systems generate systems, and how the act of making becomes a machine in itself.

The piece uses bilingual wordplay and typographic experimentation to examine themes of automation, creation, and the cyclical nature of technological development. It questions what happens when the machine becomes both the means and the end of production.
