*Our Generation: Programs + Computer-Generated Texts* is a collection edited by Nick Montfort and published by Bad Quarto, featuring contemporary computational poetry and digital literature. The collection showcases how contemporary writers are using code, algorithms, and computational processes to generate new forms of poetry and text. 

**Format:** 8.5" × 5.5", color cardstock cover + 24 pp.
