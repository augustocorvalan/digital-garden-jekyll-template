'Otros Nosotros' (Us and Others) is a program that prints out a poem of variable lengths. The poem begins with 'tuyo' (yours) but subsequent lines separate the letter groups 'tu' (you), 'y' (and), 'yo' (me) with increasing voids between them. The voids widen progressively until a pivotal line where the separation reaches its apex. At this point the pattern reverses, and the voids begin to close in on themselves, bringing the letter groups back together until they reunite as 'tuyo' once more.
