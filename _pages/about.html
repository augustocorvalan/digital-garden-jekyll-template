--- layout: page title: About permalink: /about/ --- Agustin Rosa is an artist, writer and editor from Rosario, Argentina. His work explores speculative storytelling, digital interfaces as well as traditional book and object making techniques. He is an editor at Dead Alive Press and Taper Magazine as well as chief curator at Sleepwalker Collective, a DIY exhibition space. He is an alumni of School for Poetic Computation and has earned a B.A. in Creative Writing from Columbia University and an M.F.A. in Studio Art from Maryland Institute College of Art.
