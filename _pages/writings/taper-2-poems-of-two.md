Taper is an online literary journal for computational poetry and literary art, published twice yearly by [Bad Quarto](https://badquar.to/).

Taper #2: Poems of Two exploresd literary and numerical themes of the number 2. Taper melds the literary traditions of the Oulipo with the restrictions of computing systems to provide a fertile ground for creativity. For this issue, we invite self-contained computational pieces that explore "two" in diverse ways: e.g. mathematical expression (doubles, halves), juxtaposition (pairs, inverses), and/or language constructions (bigrams, digrams).
