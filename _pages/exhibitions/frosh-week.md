<h3 class="uppercase">Works</h3>
<a class="internal-link" href="/quatro-bocas">Quatro Bocas</a>
<hr/>

FROSH WEEK explores the artist as a perpetual newcomer, unrefined and ready to play. Each work recalls the thrill of firsts—the first idea, the first mark, the first mistake in a variety of media. Join us as we celebrate our rudimentary selves. Bring your roommate. Wear something unwashed.
