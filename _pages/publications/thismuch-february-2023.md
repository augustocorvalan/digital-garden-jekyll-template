<h3 class="uppercase">Works</h3>
<a class="internal-link" href="/two-love-poems">Two Love Poems</a>
<hr/>

This much is an annual writing competition and publication that asks the question, “Do you know how much I love you?”. It is a project by David Alpert. He first started it when he was thinking and feeling a lot about love. He continues the project even though he is thinking and feeling about a lot of things in addition to love.
