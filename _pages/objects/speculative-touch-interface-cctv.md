As skin becomes interface, and interface becomes self we must ask: where does the machine end, and where do you begin? 

These "speculative anatomies"—digital yet corporeal, intimate yet uncanny—explore the seduction and surveillance of interfaces.
The digital fleshscape, while promising connection, wounds bodies stretched across networks of capital and code.

These paper pulp sculptures arises from the cybernetic supply chain, transforming its waste.
They form speculative anatomies composed of organs, ports, grid-like screens, and apertures that might be mouths or cameras or something else entirely.
