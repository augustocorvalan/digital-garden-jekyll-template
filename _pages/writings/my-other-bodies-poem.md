My Other Bodies (Poem)

Welcome​
​Come inside
Bite my doorknob
Bite my throat
​What's next?
​Stroke my thorns
Stroke my fruit
Stroke my sofa
Lick my lips
Lick my little finger
Boil my wires
Boil my veins
Welcome
Do you feel it?

​​Are you watching?
Closer
Eat my seeds
Eat my withered bits
Cradle my stove
Come closer
Lick my dirt
Lick my charger
​Gather the pieces of my face
Thank you
