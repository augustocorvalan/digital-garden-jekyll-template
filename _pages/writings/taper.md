Taper is an online literary magazine for small computational pieces, published twice a year by [Bad Quarto](https://badquar.to/). Each issue gathers short programs, poems and systems around a theme.

I joined the editorial collective with issue #14 and have edited each issue since. Read all issues at [taper.badquar.to](https://taper.badquar.to/).

My own work appears in earlier issues: <a class="internal-link" href="/taper-2-poems-of-two">Taper 2</a>, <a class="internal-link" href="/taper-6-throw-of-dice">Taper 6</a> and <a class="internal-link" href="/taper-12-tools">Taper 12</a>.
